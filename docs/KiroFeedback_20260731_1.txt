Assessment Review: Full Analysis and Recommendations
The Central Problem: Scale Mismatch
This is a fundamental math error that overrides everything else. The archetype coordinates in engine.js are in a large integer scale (values ranging from -16 to +16). But the scoring pipeline normalizes user answers by dividing the raw sum by the question count (25), which produces a final score in roughly [-1.9, +1.9] per axis. The distance calculation then compares a user vector like [1.4, -0.9, -1.6, 0.1, 0.0, 1.6] against archetype coordinates like [12, -4, -10, -2, -6, 10].

The coordinates are 6× larger than the scores. The squared Euclidean distances to every archetype are enormous, and the winner is determined almost entirely by which archetype has the smallest total magnitude — not by the closest conceptual match.

The Inmyfeels coordinate [-4, 4, 4, 8, 6, -4] has a squared distance of 164 from the origin. Every other archetype is farther. This means any user with a score near [0,0,0,0,0,0] — which is the average of mixed answers — routes to Inmyfeels. The self-routing test confirms this: 11 out of 12 archetypes route to Inmyfeels when you simulate selecting answers that best match that archetype.

Consequences:

Inmyfeels is the black hole of the assessment. Probably 60-70% of actual users are getting it.
The other 11 archetypes are largely inaccessible.
All of the question work, vector tuning, and coordinate design is wasted while this mismatch exists.
Root Cause Diagnosis
The comment block in engine.js says:

+2 = strong positive pole, +1 = moderate positive pole, 0 = neutral...

But the actual coordinates look like [12, -4, -10, -2, -6, 10]. Someone manually multiplied the intended [2, -0.67, -1.67, -0.33, -1, 1.67] values by approximately 6 during a calibration pass, but the normalization divisor wasn't updated to match. The normalization still divides by questionCount (25) instead of questionCount / 6 (≈4.17), so the comparison is off by a factor of 6.

There are two valid fixes:

Fix A — Scale down the coordinates (recommended): Divide all archetype coordinates by 6. Keeps the question vectors and normalization exactly as they are. Single change to one file.

Fix B — Change normalization: Multiply the normalization output by 6 before comparison. Effectively the same but confuses the diagnostic output.

Fix A is cleaner. The coordinates should be in the [-2, +2] range to match the intended design.
